Before there was a wedding business, there was a love for horses. And at the beginning of our story was Sharda.

Where our story began

It started with Sharda, not a business plan.

In 1999, our father, Shri Narayan Mali, brought home his first mare, Sharda, in Udaipur. He was passionate about horses, and at that time, starting a wedding horse rental business was not the plan.

As the years passed, more horses became part of our family. Some were purchased, while others were born and raised under our care. Our father spent his time looking after them and preparing young horses for riding.

Then, in 2001, our family received its first wedding booking. That was the beginning of Narayan Mali Ghodi Wale as a wedding service.

Over the years, the work grew, but the connection with our horses remained at the heart of everything. Today, our father and brother continue to manage the family business.

One mare. One passion. The beginning of our family’s journey.
